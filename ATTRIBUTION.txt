NIGHTINGALE — ATTRIBUTION
=========================
Nightingale 48-hour build. Jason Ong Yann Jing, September 2026.

All patient data in this project is SYNTHETIC. No real clinic, no real
clinicians, no real patients. "Harmoni Medical Centre" is invented, and its
website corpus in db/seed/clinic-corpus.json was written for this build.

MODELS
------
Google Gemini 3.5 Flash             Google AI Studio API, commercial terms
  Conversational replies. Downgrades to Flash Lite on quota exhaustion.

Google Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite        Google AI Studio API, commercial terms
  Risk classification and structured fact extraction. Runs on every message.

No model was fine-tuned or trained. No patient data was used for training. All
model calls receive REDACTED text only — see lib/redaction/pipeline.ts.

NOTE ON DATA RESIDENCY: the AI Studio endpoint accepts no region parameter, so
LLM_REGION records intent rather than routing. Production would use Vertex AI in
asia-southeast1. Documented as a known limitation in the Technical Brief rather
than hidden.


CLINICAL SOURCES
----------------
The red-flag lexicon in config/red_flags.yaml is derived from published triage
protocols. No protocol text is reproduced; the phrases are lay-language
renderings, and every rule carries its source in the `source` field.

  Manchester Triage System (MTS)              red-flag structure
  Emergency Severity Index (ESI)              acuity definitions
  WHO Emergency Triage Assessment (ETAT)      paediatric red flags
  NHS 111 / NHS Pathways                      red-flag phrasing
  FAST criteria                               stroke recognition
